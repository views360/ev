Welcome!

This tool helps you compare EV charging subscription costs with average PAYG rates.

Comparison Modes

Switch between two modes:

  1. BREAK EVEN (default)
  2. TRIP SAVINGS.

BREAK EVEN

Break Even (default mode) displays all subscription providers in break-even-miles order. The break-even-miles value shows how many miles you must drive using a provider's discounted rate to recoup its subscription fee.

TRIP SAVINGS

Trip Savings mode calculates potential savings for a planned trip. Fill in all fields to view results.

Assessing Results

The results table at the bottom of the page will automatically update when you fill in or change field values.

If you are unsure what a field is asking for, hover over (or click) the💡for more information.

Don't forget that subscriptions last an entire month.
