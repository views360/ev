Welcome to the EV Subscription Comparison (UK) Tool

Going on a long trip and want to know if a one-month EV charging subscription will save you money over PAYG rates?

This tool will tell you!

Comparison Modes

Switch between two modes:

  1. BREAK EVEN (default)
  2. TRIP SAVINGS.

BREAK EVEN

Break Even (default mode) displays a break-even-miles value for all subscription providers. This tells you how many miles you must drive using a provider's discounted rate to recoup its subscription fee.

TRIP SAVINGS

Trip Savings mode calculates potential savings for a planned trip.

Assessing Results

After completing all fields (which you may edit on the fly), the results table at the bottom of the page will automatically update.

If you are unsure what a field is asking for, hover over (or click) the💡for more information.
