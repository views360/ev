Optimising Your EV Charging Strategy

Welcome to EV Subs Compare, a dedicated utility designed to help UK electric vehicle drivers navigate the increasingly complex landscape of public charging costs. As the UK transitions toward electric mobility, major networks like Tesla, IONITY, and BP Pulse have introduced subscription models that offer significant discounts in exchange for a fixed monthly fee.

Our Mission

The goal of this tool is to provide total transparency. Choosing between a standard Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) rate and a monthly subscription can be difficult without accurate data. We provide a data-driven approach to help you decide if a one-month subscription is a savvy financial move for your specific driving habits.

Why Use This Tool?

Public charging costs can vary wildly, from 40p/kWh to over 85p/kWh. A subscription might cost £10 but lower your rate by 20p/kWh. This means if you charge more than 50kWh in a month, you've already saved money. Our tool automates this calculation across all major UK providers instantly.

How the Comparisons Work

  • Break-Even Analysis: Calculates the exact mileage required to recoup a subscription fee based on your car's efficiency.
  • Trip Savings: Perfect for planning long-distance holidays, showing you exactly how much you'll save on a specific route.

Designed for Privacy

We believe in a "privacy-first" approach. This tool does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. We use local cookies solely to remember your vehicle settings so that the tool is ready to use immediately upon your next visit.
