About This Tool

Deciding between a Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) rate and a monthly subscription can be difficult without accurate data. We provide a data-driven approach to help you decide if a one-month subscription is a savvy financial move for your specific driving habits.

Why Use This Tool?

Public charging costs can vary wildly, from 40p/kWh to over 85p/kWh. A subscription might cost £10 but lower your rate by 20p/kWh. Our tool automates this calculation across all known UK subscription providers instantly.

How the Comparisons Work

  • Break-Even Analysis: Calculates the exact mileage required to recoup a subscription fee based on your car's efficiency.
  • Trip Savings: Perfect for planning long-distance holidays, showing you exactly how much you'll save on a specific route.

Designed for Privacy

We believe in a privacy-first approach. This tool does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. We use local cookies solely to remember your vehicle settings so that the tool is ready to use immediately upon your next visit.
