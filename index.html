Calculation Type

Switch between two calculation types:

  1. BREAK EVEN (default)
  2. TRIP SAVINGS.

BREAK EVEN

Break Even (default calculation type) displays the break-even-miles value for all subscription providers. This tells you how many miles you must drive using a provider's discounted rate to recoup its subscription fee.

TRIP SAVINGS

TTrip Savings dives deeper into your specifics to calculate potential savings for a planned trip.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or stored by this tool.

It uses cookies solely to save the values you enter into the input fields. This is done for your convenience so your data is available for subsequent visits.

Important Reminder About the Break-Even Calculation Type

The break-even distance is the number of miles you must drive to recoup a provider's monthly subscription fee. This is a product of your stated vehicle efficiency (miles/kWh) and your average expected PAYG rate when not using a subscription.

Important: the break-even miles begins when you first charge with a provider — not when you set off from home with a pre-charged battery.
