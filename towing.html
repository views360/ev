1. The 30–50% Rule: Planning for Half Your Normal Range

The most important adjustment when towing a caravan or trailer is managing your range.

  • Typical range loss: Expect to lose 30–50% of your usual mileage.
  • Weight vs. Drag: While a heavy trailer impacts acceleration, the shape of the caravan affects high-speed efficiency more.
Rule of thumb: Halve your official WLTP range for comfortable planning when towing a full-sized caravan.

2. Type-Approval: Can Your EV Tow at All?
